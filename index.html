My story

I'm Limited, a 15-year-old developer with a growing interest in web development, games, and the communities built around them. I create websites and contribute to projects in the OGFN community, including work with AerisOGFN, Zerel, and Vero. I've been Muslim my whole life. I speak Dutch, Polish, and English, and a little Turkish. Alongside developing, I'm learning C++ and C# and exploring game development with Unity and Unreal Engine 5.

Before finding my place in OGFN, I spent time supporting several Fortnite esports teams. I also owned an esports team in 2023 and was part of Nova Esports, which shut down in 2025. Those experiences were an early introduction to building and supporting online communities.

I joined the OGFN community in mid-September 2025. My first emulator project was Moon. Over time, it was rebranded as Solar, then Mowind, and later Lumora. That project eventually shut down, but I decided to take what I learned and start fresh rather than give up on the idea.

That fresh start became Relive, a project I own and develop that has grown to a community of more than 1,300 members. I've also donated to charity a few times, because giving back is important to me. Outside of development, I love math and PE, and my favorite foods are French fries and döner kebab. I'm continuing to build Relive while developing my skills, learning from each project, and looking for new ways to contribute , i used to be an pc tweaker that gave people free windows tweaks long ago when i didnt know alot about computers.
